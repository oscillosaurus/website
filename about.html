OSCILLOSAURUS / ABOUT

Oscillosaurus.

Australian Synth DIY since 2015.

About

Synth DIY from Australia.

Oscillosaurus is an Australian Synth DIY project creating Eurorack modules, front panels and open-source hardware.

Originally active through the mid-to-late 2010s, Oscillosaurus produced original modules alongside an extensive range of black-and-gold front panels for the DIY modular community.

After a lengthy interruption, the project is active again.

History

A brief history of Oscillosaurus.

2015

Oscillosaurus begins.

Front panels, Synth DIY projects and the first Oscillosaurus hardware.

2016

Massive Passive / Crosscaler

Compact passive Eurorack utilities join the Oscillosaurus range.

2017-2019

SSOMA, Superbooth and TFOM

SSOMA is introduced and Oscillosaurus heads to Superbooth in Berlin. SSOMA and later EPOCH gets presenting in Japan at the Tokyo Festival of Modular.

2015—2021

More Front Panels / 0HP gadgets

An expanding catalogue of black-and-gold panels for Barton Musical Circuits, Mutable Instruments and other Synth DIY projects.

2026

Oscillosaurus returns.

Same dinosaur. New ideas.

The Human

Made by Gareth.

Oscillosaurus is the work of Gareth Johnson.

It grew from an interest in modular synthesis, electronics, graphic design and making things that probably didn't need to exist.

Panels were designed in Australia and manufactured in small batches, with Oscillosaurus eventually finding its way into modular systems around the world.

The revived Oscillosaurus continues in much the same spirit: small-run hardware, useful oddities, open-source projects and a slightly unreasonable amount of black and gold.

Philosophy

DIY is the point.

Oscillosaurus has always existed alongside the Synth DIY community.

Many of the projects are based around open-source hardware, community-designed circuits and projects intended to be built, modified and understood.

Where possible, documentation, design files and source material are preserved here — including projects that are no longer available.
